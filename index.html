Where it comes from

We buy in small lots directly from mills and cooperatives in Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, Indonesia, and Brazil, and we publish the process — washed, natural, or wet-hulled — on every bag. If a lot sells out, it doesn't come back until next season.

About the roastery

Ember & Third is a small roastery. We roast twice a week in batches under 20lb, cup every batch before it ships, and print the roast date on the bag — not a "best by" date eight months out.
